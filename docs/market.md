# Market

Researched October 6, 2026.

## Size

Influencer marketing is roughly $32-40B in 2026, depending on the report and
on whether platform software and services are counted.

## Demand

Pay-per-view creator programs are already large. Whop's Content Rewards
reports 3.5B+ clipped views in a month. Most campaigns pay $0.50-1.50 per
1,000 views, and UGC campaigns pay more than clipping.

## Competition

- Agentio: raised $40M at a $340M valuation in November 2025. Serves
  enterprise brands buying paid creator media, starting from YouTube.
- Whop, Vyro, Clipping.io: marketplaces that supply creators and payment rails.
- Sideshift: UGC platform that lets a brand onboard creators at scale.

## The gap

Nobody operates the program for the mid-size brand running one out of Discord
and a spreadsheet. The marketplaces give that brand creators and a way to pay
them. Someone still has to recruit, brief, review, track and announce.

## Ideas considered and dropped

- AI outbound sales agency
- Speculative websites for local businesses
- AI search visibility audits

All three are crowded and we have no edge in them.
