# Competitors and features to copy

Desk research on October 7, 2026, from product pages, docs and pricing
pages. Wording is paraphrased. "Not stated" means the page did not say.

## Who already does this

| Company | What it is | Price to the brand |
|---|---|---|
| viral.app | Tracking, campaigns and payouts for app brands. The closest full tool | $99 or $229 a month plus 3% of payouts. Managed service from $20k a month |
| Whop Content Rewards | Marketplace for clipping and UGC campaigns. Brand funds a budget | Fee not published by Whop |
| Vyro | Clipping marketplace. Creators link accounts and paste post links | Not published |
| Launchpoint | Runs creator campaigns end to end for brands and agencies | Flat fee per creator, amount not stated |
| Sideshift | UGC hiring marketplace plus program tool | $299, $499 or $999 a month. Managed from $20k a month |
| Shortimize | Tracking first: paste an account, every new video is captured | $99 or $249 a month |
| FindClout | Managed clipping network at enterprise scale | Per CPM, pilots at $20k to $30k |
| Clipping.io | Clipping network, performance based | Not stated |

Classic influencer tools (Modash, Insense, GRIN, Passionfroot, Agentio) are
less relevant: none pay per view.

## Features to copy, most valuable first

| # | Feature | Who has it | Size for us | Status here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Creator submission page: a public link where a creator pastes a post and sees its status and earnings | Whop, Vyro, viral.app, Launchpoint | Medium | Missing |
| 2 | Scheduled view re-sync with a counting window, pay pending until it closes | viral.app, Shortimize, Vyro | Medium | Manual refresh only |
| 3 | Submission queue with statuses and a 48 hour auto-approve | Whop, Vyro, Sideshift | Small | Statuses exist, no timer |
| 4 | Payout rule engine: tiers, bonuses, caps | viral.app, Whop, Launchpoint | Medium | One rate, one cap, one minimum |
| 5 | Budget ledger: total, committed, paid, remaining | Whop | Small | Missing |
| 6 | Public apply link with a requests queue | viral.app, Sideshift, Whop | Medium | Missing |
| 7 | Tracked accounts or hashtag, so posts are captured without a link | Shortimize, viral.app, Launchpoint, Vyro | Medium | Hashtag check built |
| 8 | Leaderboard of top creators and top posts | FindClout, Clipping.io, Shortimize | Small | Roster ranking only |
| 9 | Ban for botting, duplicate check, creator trust score | Whop, Launchpoint, FindClout, Vyro | Small | Per-post flags only |
| 10 | Payout simulation and CSV export | viral.app | Small | Missing |

No competitor publishes how it detects fake views, so showing our signals
per creator is a visible edge.

## Not copying for the MVP

- Moving money, identity checks and tax forms: everyone hands this to a payout partner.
- A creator marketplace or database: supply is their moat and takes years.
- OAuth account linking: needs platform app approval.
- Contracts and e-signature: legal surface, little demo value.
- More platforms: each one is a separate scraper. TikTok only.
- Holding the brand's budget: that is holding client money.
